Overview

What it is

ValuationEngine.value_stock(ticker, as_of_date) is the single entry point every screen calls. It loads point-in-time fundamentals and a macro risk-free rate, classifies the company's lifecycle stage, picks the DCF variant that stage implies, computes WACC, and returns one ValuationResult — intrinsic value, gap vs. market price, margin of safety, and every intermediate assumption, so nothing on screen is an unexplained number.

damodaran_valuation/lifecycle

Lifecycle classification

LifecycleClassifier assigns a company to a stage first, because the "right" DCF model and terminal growth rate depend entirely on where a company sits in its life — a hyper-growth company and a declining one shouldn't share a valuation template.

Terminal growth rate is stage-dependent (0.02–0.06), not a flat assumption — the sensitivity grid centers on this stage-specific base case rather than a hardcoded 5%, since a DECLINING-stage stock's 2% base case would otherwise be misrepresented by a fixed anchor.

DCF models

ModelWhen it's used
FCFFTwoStageModelStandard high-growth-then-terminal two-stage free-cash-flow-to-firm model — the default for most lifecycle stages
FCFFThreeStageModelAdds a transitional middle stage for companies whose growth won't step down cleanly in one jump
ExcessReturnModelValues based on return on invested capital vs. cost of capital, for stable/mature companies where FCFF projection is noisier than ROIC-based value creation
CommodityNormalizedModelNormalizes cyclical commodity-linked earnings before projecting, for sectors where trailing fundamentals alone would misrepresent normal earning power

WACCCalculator computes the discount rate from real inputs — cost of equity (risk-free rate + beta × equity risk premium), cost of debt, and capital-structure weights — rather than a flat assumed rate per sector.

damodaran_valuation/relative

Relative valuation

RelativePERegression regresses trailing PE against growth, ROE, and risk proxies across the peer set, so a stock's "fair" multiple is derived from what the market is actually paying for similar fundamentals right now — not a static sector-average PE.

Robustness

Sensitivity grid & Monte Carlo

A single intrinsic-value point estimate hides how sensitive it is to two of the most debatable inputs in any DCF: the discount rate and the terminal growth assumption.

ToolWhat it does
GET /valuation/{ticker}/sensitivityRe-runs the DCF at every combination of WACC ±1–5 steps of 1% and terminal growth ±1–5 steps of 1% around the stage-specific base case, returning the full grid of intrinsic values
MonteCarloDCFSamples across input uncertainty to produce a distribution of intrinsic values rather than one number, and a probability the stock is undervalued at the current price
3-scenario framingBull / base / bear intrinsic values with probability weights, shown alongside the point estimate
Always displayed, never skipped. The sensitivity grid isn't an optional drill-down — every valuation response is expected to carry it, so a single confident-looking number never ships without its uncertainty band next to it.

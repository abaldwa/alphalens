Overview

What it is

Two consumers of the same underlying idea, deliberately kept separate rather than blended into one score: M-09 is pure, auditable formula computation — no training, no black box, every number traceable to a named academic model. M-10 is a learned ensemble that can pick up patterns the formulas can't name. A stock's forensic risk profile shows both, so a red flag from the formula side can't be silently overridden by the ML side or vice versa.

M-09 — SPEC-MODEL-009

Classical Forensic Scores

systems/ml_signal_engine/models/forensic/classical_scores.py — 7 independently-testable pure functions, each operating on explicit named inputs derived from real quarterly fundamentals by features/forensic_classical.py. "No training required — pure formula computation" per spec.

ModelWhat it flags
Beneish M-Score8 components (DSRI, GMI, AQI, SGI, DEPI, SGAI, LVGI, TATA) + composite — probability of earnings manipulation
Altman Z-Score5 components + composite — bankruptcy/distress risk
Piotroski F-Score9 binary components → 0–9 — fundamental financial strength
Ohlson O-ScoreProbability of financial distress; uses log(TA) directly since no Indian GNP deflator series is ingested (documented approximation, doesn't affect relative ordering)
Dechow F-ScoreComposite probability of accounting misstatement
Sloan AccrualAccrual-based earnings-quality ratio
Benford's Law5 features — chi-squared deviation of leading-digit distribution across revenue/expense/receivables from the expected Benford distribution
Same-quarter-last-year comparisons. Every "t-1" term in Beneish/Piotroski/Ohlson/Dechow compares to the same quarter one year ago (t-4 in quarterly cadence), not the immediately preceding quarter — controls for seasonality, matching how revenue_growth_yoy is computed elsewhere in the codebase.
M-10

Forensic ML Ensemble

ForensicMLModel (forensic_ml.py) trains on the 54 deep forensic features (Phase 3 catalogue) plus market-microstructure signals it derives itself — price/volume divergence, abnormal-return reversal, short-interest proxy from F&O history — none of which the classical formulas can see.

Deep forensic groupCountWhat it captures
B — Cash flow quality deep9Beyond the basic CFO/accrual ratios — deeper cash-flow-statement forensics
C — Revenue quality deep7Revenue-recognition red flags beyond Beneish's SGI/GMI
D — Balance sheet quality12Goodwill/asset ratios, intangibles, contingent liabilities
E — India governance & promoter risk15Promoter remuneration %, related-party-transaction growth, board independence, auditor flags, director churn, CFO tenure, pledge spiral risk
H — Cross-validation & consistency10Tax-rate consistency, employee cost, statutory dues, segment consistency

compute_governance_score() folds the India-specific governance group into a single number the model consumes alongside the raw feature vector, and _flag_for_score() maps the model's output probability onto the same red/amber/green flag language the classical side uses, so the two layers are visually comparable even though they're computed independently.
